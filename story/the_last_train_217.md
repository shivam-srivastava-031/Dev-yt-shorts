**Audience:** US, general
**Style:** Flat 2D cut-out animation (simple shapes, bold outlines, limited moves), so one beginner animator can make it on a laptop.

## 2. Narration script (124 words)

> **The man behind Maya didn't exist. She could only see him in the window.**
>
> 2:17 AM. Two trains pulled in, side by side. Exhausted, Maya jumped on the closest one.
>
> Her car was empty. Just the hum of the rails, and her tired reflection.
>
> Then she saw him. A man in a yellow raincoat, right behind her. She turned. Empty seat.
>
> The lights flickered. In the glass, he was closer, pressing his phone to the window.
>
> Two words. *Wrong train.* Above the door, the sign read: *Not in service.*
>
> The tracks split. He was never behind her. He was on the other train.
>
> Hers rolled into the dark yard. Doors locked. The last train at 2:17 left on time.
>
> **Maya just wasn't on it.**

Delivery: low, close to the mic, unhurried. Leave a beat after "Empty seat." and after "Doors locked." Say "two seventeen A M."

**Fair-play clues for the twist** (all planted before the reveal):
- Scene 2: the near train's side sign says NOT IN SERVICE, and its windows are dark. A yellow raincoat is visible in the far train.
- Scenes 1, 4 and 5: a faint warm rectangle (the other train's window) sits behind the man, and he sways out of rhythm with Maya's car.

## 8. Final twist and closing line

**Twist:** Nothing supernatural happened. Two trains left at 2:17 side by side. Maya, half asleep, boarded the near one: an out-of-service train headed to the rail yard. The "man behind her" was a passenger on the real last train. He was visible through two panes of glass and a foot of darkness, so he looked like a reflection standing over her shoulder. He saw her board the empty train and tried to warn her. When the tracks split, his train went downtown and hers went to the yard to be locked up for the night.

**Closing line:** *"The last train at 2:17 left on time. Maya just wasn't on it."*

## How to remake it with Krita, OpenToonz, Audacity and Shotcut

The build script draws every layer separately and saves it to `assets/`, so each tool has something to start from.

1. **Krita (art).** Open the PNGs in `assets/art/` and paint over them in your own style. Keep each background and each character pose on its own layer. Export as PNG with transparency, using the same file names.
2. **OpenToonz (animation).** Import the character PNGs as levels. Rig Maya's head on a pivot at the neck (for the Scene 3 sway and the Scene 4 head turn). Use the Xsheet timings in the table above at 30 fps. Hand-animate only three things: the door slide, Maya's walk bob, and the man's flicker jumps (just swap scale and position on blackout frames). Everything else is camera moves.
3. **Audacity (voice and sound).** `assets/audio/narration/*.wav` is a scratch voice (Windows TTS). Record your own read line by line over it, then apply Noise Reduction, a High-Pass Filter at 80 Hz, Compressor, and Normalize to −1 dB. Export WAV. `sfx_track.wav` and `music_track.wav` are separate stems.
4. **Shotcut (edit).** Use 1080×1920 at 30 fps. Put the video on V1, narration on A1, SFX on A2 and music on A3 (about −14 dB, ducked under the voice). Add captions with a Text filter, using the timings from the build log. Export H.264 MP4 with AAC at 192 kbps.
